Amazon-specific re-signing is acceptable in principle; cross-store install-over is a documented limitation rather than a mandatory acceptance criterion. Preserve the existing itch package and key, verify within-channel updates and warn about signature incompatibility; never require uninstall or clear data. If switching channels, offer only tested portable backup/restore with documented limits. Any package change requires approval. Google Play signing strategy, stable long-term identity and update continuity are higher priority. Keep privacy, safety, data-loss and platform submission requirements binding. Do not promise continued Amazon updates; decide support and listing disposition explicitly before publication.
