## Information and data handling in the current app

**Offline play:** GridMates: Sudoku can play Solo puzzles and its locally generated Daily puzzle without signing in, creating an account or connecting to a hosted game server. The app saves player-selected curated display name, randomly assigned local installation identifier, puzzle state, pencil marks, hints/assistance, completions, achievements, cosmetic unlocks, preferences and recent play statistics in the app's private local storage. This data is needed to continue games and show rewards.

**Nearby multiplayer:** Co-op and Versus use a direct TCP connection on shared Wi-Fi. A participating device hosts the room and the other connects to it. Your chosen display name, appearance, game state, approved Quick Chat messages, emote identifiers and (in Co-op only) temporary cell attention markers can be transmitted to your connected partner. Display names consist of two or three words selected from a fixed English catalog; some words unlock with achievements. The Beta 3 source provides no unrestricted name or chat text entry. Older custom profile names are replaced with a curated starter name when necessary; this does not reset puzzle progress or earned rewards. Nearby discovery advertises a generic GridMates room label rather than a player name, and receiving devices generate the label from a validated room identifier instead of displaying arbitrary advertisement text. Discovery still exposes a room identifier, local network endpoint, protocol version and room-code requirement to nearby devices before joining. Connected devices necessarily use local network addresses. Curated display names are exchanged after joining and may be retained in saved multiplayer room state. Reconnection credentials are exchanged with the participating device and stored locally to let a reserved partner rejoin. These credentials are not account passwords and should not be shared.

The host retains up to 30 recent approved messages/reactions in room memory, including for same-partner reconnection; replacing the partner or closing the room clears this history. Partner Ping markers are not saved or included in reconnect history. Players may temporarily hide incoming messages, reactions, unread indicators and explicit markers; hiding communication does not erase temporary room history or prevent ordinary partner selection from being shown. The current LAN game protocol is not encrypted. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network. Room codes are enabled by default for users without a saved preference; players can change this setting. Room codes restrict admission but do not encrypt traffic. No developer-operated matchmaking or internet-wide account service is identified in this version. These statements describe updated Beta 3 source and require verification against the exact delivered build, not earlier public builds.

**Network access and permissions:** The checked Android manifest declares internet access, network state, Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast permissions for connectivity and nearby room discovery. Internet permission is required for network sockets even when playing on a local network. The checked manifest does not request contacts, camera, microphone or location permissions. The app uses a non-exported FileProvider to share user-selected files when needed. Verify the merged manifest and actual network behavior of the distributed package before publication.

**App operator servers:** The current build has no developer-operated gameplay server for storing puzzle results, player accounts or nearby chat. We should confirm third-party dependency behavior and the actual release APK before asserting a comprehensive "no collection" position for store privacy disclosures.

**Local backup and restore:** Settings allows you to export an optional progress-backup file. You choose where to save/share that file; if you send it to a separate service, that service's privacy terms apply. It includes Solo/Daily progress, match records, cosmetic unlock ownership, earned achievement IDs (which determine collected name words), the selected display name and preferences, but **not active host/guest multiplayer rooms or reconnection credentials**. Restore asks for an explicit confirmation and verifies imported data before replacing local progress.

**Retention and control:** Local app data is retained to continue gameplay. The app may prune older match records and retains earned achievement and cosmetic ownership separately. It also maintains private recovery copies of progression and may preserve damaged save files to avoid accidental data loss. Android app backup is disabled in the checked source. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local progression and recovery files. A manual backup should be saved in a safe location *before* switching devices or uninstalling. We don't offer account-based cloud recovery in the current release. You can delete local game data using Android app settings, subject to your saved external backup files being under your control. Other players may have their own copies of information you shared in a nearby game.

**Advertising, in-app purchases, analytics:** The checked source includes no advertising, billing or analytics SDK integration. If any of those systems are added later, this policy and any relevant platform disclosures need revision before the updated version is published. We should verify the shipped dependency manifest and live APK as part of release QA.

**Store distribution:** If you obtain the game through Amazon Appstore or another storefront, that storefront may separately process information for its own account, delivery and platform services. Its privacy notices apply to those services. The exact store-delivered package and disclosures must be reviewed before this draft is published.
