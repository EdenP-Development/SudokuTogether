## Art source

An image generator produced the transparent atlas. Its four isolated sprites were cropped and centered on 384 × 384 transparent canvases for the existing avatar renderer. [Garden PNGs](../app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/)

> Use case: illustration-story. Create one square transparent 2-by-2 game sprite sheet for four friendly insect profile portraits in a cozy paperback Sudoku app. Top left a honeybee with muted honey-gold and dark brown bands and two cream wings; top right a red-orange ladybug with three readable dark spots on each wing cover; bottom left a butter-yellow and dusky plum butterfly with broad rounded symmetrical wings; bottom right a teal dragonfly with four broad pale blue wings and a short striped tail. Each is a separate self-contained centered front-facing full silhouette, as legible at 48 pixels as a simple animal avatar, with round dot eyes and one restrained smile. Use the visual language of a flat printed cottage field guide sticker: softly rounded forms, very clean solid fills, a uniform dark forest green outline around the outer silhouette, cream highlights, almost no tonal texture and zero dimensional effects. Keep all four portraits similarly weighted with wide transparent spaces between and around them, each confined to its own quadrant with at least 8% margin inside the quadrant. Genuine alpha transparent background between and around the insects, no frames, pedestals, text, numbers, shadows, scenery, gradients or white borders. Use generous smooth shape geometry and make each species recognizable from the silhouette.
